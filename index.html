Built PRISM: a 7-layer cognitive reasoning framework (Assume, Delete, Distort, Superposition, Conceptualize, Skin in the Game, Red Team) with a Critic Layer backward pass, ICL anchors, and confidence scoring. Self-designed from scratch.

Applied to a 3–5 year career direction analysis — produced a structured intelligence brief identifying the strategic path, surfacing three hidden constraints, and documenting the full reasoning trail in Google Drive as a deliverable.
